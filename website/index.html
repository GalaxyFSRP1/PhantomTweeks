The Phantom Pipeline

Measure first. Change second. Prove it worked.

Most "FPS boosters" apply the same registry list to every machine and call it a day. Phantom Tweeks runs a real engineering loop instead.

1

Scan & Analyze

Reads your actual CPU, GPU, memory, storage, drivers, startup load and network — never assumptions from a spec sheet.

2

Recommend

Each suggestion carries a technical reason and a confidence rating. If a tweak won't help your configuration, you get "No change recommended."

3

Backup & Approve

Nothing is written until a verified backup exists and you have ticked the box. Automatic mode is off by default.

4

Benchmark & Compare

Captures real frame times before and after, then reports the delta honestly — including when the delta is zero.

5

Restore If Necessary

If a benchmark shows a regression, Phantom Tweeks tells you and offers to roll the change straight back.

The Golden Rule

Phantom Tweeks must never break your PC in an attempt to make it faster.

Given a choice between +2 FPS and possible instability, it chooses stability. Given a choice between claiming a win and reporting no measurable change, it reports the truth.

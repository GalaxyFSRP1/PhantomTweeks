PHANTOM TWEEKS — BEFORE YOU INSTALL

Phantom Tweeks is a performance analysis and optimization tool for
Windows 10 and Windows 11.

WHAT IT DOES
  • Measures your hardware, storage, network and frame times.
  • Recommends changes, each with a technical reason and a confidence rating.
  • Applies only what you approve, after writing a verified backup.
  • Lets you roll back everything with one click.

WHAT IT WILL NEVER DO
  • Close or suspend your game, Steam, Discord, Epic, OBS or anti-cheat.
  • Disable Windows Defender, Firewall, Windows Update or security mitigations.
  • Modify game files, executables or anti-cheat software.
  • Delete your personal files.
  • Overclock, undervolt, or disable CPU cores.
  • Claim an improvement it did not measure.

If a tweak is unlikely to help your specific hardware, Phantom Tweeks will
tell you "No change recommended." That is the product working correctly.

Your data stays on your PC. There is no telemetry in this build.
